### Dungeon Evade



##### Description



Dungeon Evade is a 3D horror escape game set inside an abandoned warehouse. Your objective is to explore the randomly generated warehouse, collect all five keys, avoid the Nextbots chasing you, and find your way to the exit.



Each run can have a different warehouse layout, so you'll need to pay attention to your surroundings. Along the way, you may also come across treasure chests. Opening one can reward you with an extra life or a speed boost, but it could also trigger a trap or slow you down. Choose wisely!
